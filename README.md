# Capstone Competition

## Business Problem Statement

Swire Coca Cola experiences a loss of approximately 60 million dollars a year due to machine down times annually. Currently workers are deployed in a reactive fashion to fix the broken machines but this is a slow process that limits productivity. A solution to this problem would be implementing a predictive model that could alert workers of machines with a high probability of malfunctioning prior to the incident itself thus reducing the time the machine is down and increasing productivity.

## Our Solution

Our group's solution for this problem was to identify the lifespan of unique pieces of equipment throughout the production process of Swire Coca Cola's canning and bottling operation. This would allow for Swire plant managers to stock necessary parts proactively in order to plan maintenance before breakdowns can occur. The reason for this plan of attack was the large difference in downtime between Planned versus Unplanned maintenance activiy, thus saving Swire time and money.

Ultimately, I feel as though I did a great job in keeping the group focused and on task, ensuring we reached our goals on time and to the best of our abilities. 

## Value to Business

The value for the business from this analysis is in the form of time saved by reducing the number of unplanned maintenance events that take place in the various production plants across Swire's operation. These unplanned maintenance events prevent Swire from meeting the demands in terms of production as machines are forced to shut down in order for repairs to be made. By identifying when specific peices of equipment along throughout the system are getting close to breaking down, maintenance can be carried out in a timely manner when it makes the most sense for the company. This will significantly reduce down time for the company, allowing them to meet their demand, and ultimately cut into the 60 million loses Swire is experiencing at this point in time. 

## Difficulties Faced

The main difficulty we faced in this project was the data available to us was less than ideal, with the majority of the data missing and practically useless. We were forced to impute large portions of the data set in order to meet our goals, but were able to do so with a relatively high degree of accuracy. 

## Key Takeaways

My main takeaway from this project was just how difficult problems like this are to solve. This project was a great opportunity for me to really see how an analytics team functions throughout an entire project, from identifying what the problem really is, what the possible solutions are, iterating through solutions to ultimately find the best option, and most importantly, how to translate analytical results into real actionable changes that improve the outcomes for the business.
